MyFaith shows prayer times and calls you to prayer with the adhan. No ads, no subscriptions, no sign-up. Everything except listening to reciters works offline.

<b>Prayer times</b>
• Calculated for your location or for a city from a list of 34,000
• 22 calculation methods, Hanafi and Shafi Asr, per-prayer corrections in minutes
• Hijri date and a countdown to the next prayer
• Two home-screen widgets: the next prayer and the day's timetable

<b>Adhan and alarms</b>
• Adhan for each prayer, a separate adhan for Fajr, an optional early reminder
• The volume buttons do not cut the adhan off; stop it with the button in the notification
• Suhoor alarm for Ramadan, Mondays and Thursdays, the white days, or every day
• A reminder before Fajr that it is time to finish eating
• Tahajjud alarm at the start of the last third of the night or a set time before Fajr

<b>Quran</b>
• Uthmani Arabic text with a translation and the Al-Mukhtasar tafsir for every ayah
• A 604-page mushaf that turns right to left
• Color-coded tajweed and transliteration
• Search, bookmarks, and continue reading where you stopped
• Ayah-by-ayah recitation by well-known reciters, with repeat, auto-advance, and playback speed (needs internet)

<b>Remembrance and learning</b>
• Salawat, dhikr, and duas with a counter and a daily goal; every card names its source
• The 99 Names of Allah: a list and a quiz
• A step-by-step prayer guide following the Hanafi school
• Hadith of the day from Sahih al-Bukhari
• Qibla compass

Languages: Russian, English, Arabic. Dark theme included.

The app collects no data. Your location is used only to calculate prayer times and the qibla, and it never leaves your phone.
